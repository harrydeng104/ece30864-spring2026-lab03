(a) Two pros of Agile development are:
        1. Resilience to changes in scope or requirements
        2. Constant additional value to the customer
    Two cons of Agile development are:
        1. Lack of predictability because planning is not fully completed at the beginning of the project
        2. Additional time spent planning sprints and coordinating during development

(b) The role of the Scrum Master is to manage scrum-related tasks such as JIRA tooling, facilitating sprints and meetings. The role of the product owner is to set the direction for the team, handle releases, and manage stakeholders.

(c) The 3 types of meetings that happen during a sprint cycle are:
    1. Sprint planning meetings
        a. What should be completed in the upcoming sprint should be discussed
        b. Creation of stories, their story points and who they are assigned to should be discussed
    2. Daily scrum/standup meetings
        a. Work completed should be discussed
        b. Blockers should be discussed
    3. Sprint retrospective meetings
        a. Completed work should be discussed
        b. Incomplete work should be discussed and moved to the backlog
